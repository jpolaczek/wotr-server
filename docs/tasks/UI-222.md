# UI-222 — Discard hand cards by clicking them, marked in red

## Goal

Client implementation: whenever the player must discard cards from their hand (a cost such as removing a
protection card, an effect, or any other reason), they click the cards in the hand window on the right; the
cards that may be discarded are marked in red like playable cards are marked in green, and the discard is
sent as soon as the selection is complete, without a confirming click.

## Context and dependencies

The owner's request of 2026-10-17. It extends UI-142's one-click excess-hand discard to every discard from
the hand and replaces "discards as a cost retain their own decision" (AGENTS, UX_SPEC) for hand discards.
The server keeps validating costs (for A Power too Great / Tom Bombadil: one Shadow die of any result plus one
Army and one Character card, `RegionProtection`).

## Logic and scope

- Discardable cards in the hand get a red highlight (the counterpart of the green "playable" one); cards
  that cannot be chosen stay unmarked.
- A click toggles a card in the selection; when the selection meets the option exactly (count and groups,
  e.g. one Army + one Character), it is sent at once. A selection that allows several sizes keeps ✓ only for
  the smaller legal sizes.
- No separate discard list in a decision window; the decision panel shows the cost and progress only.

## Acceptance criteria

- Removing A Power too Great: choose the die, click one Army and one Character card in the hand (both red);
  the second click removes the protection, spending the die.
- Every other hand discard works by clicks in the hand with red marks and no extra Confirm.

## Verification

- EditMode: selection completeness per option (count, groups); PlayMode helpers updated; visual check in
  the next release.
