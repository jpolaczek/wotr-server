# UI-215 — Card figure choices in the region panel

## Goal

Client implementation: figure choices of one army (Denethor's Folly, Stormcrow loss, The Grey Company, Hill-Trolls, Rage of the Dunlendings units), Dead Men companions and excess figures after a card are made in the region panel with eligible figures highlighted; ✓ in the console.

## Context and dependencies

User request 2026-10-16: card choices about "what goes where" are handled in the fixed UI at the bottom with a Confirm choice option; one click on a highlighted figure, then distribute it on the board while the rules allow; fewest windows and clicks. Design: [CARD_CHOICES_UX](wotr-client:docs/ui/CARD_CHOICES_UX.md). Today a region click fills figure buttons in the decision window.

## Logic and scope

- Eligible regions are highlighted; a click opens the region panel (RegionHoverPanel) with the eligible figures highlighted and selectable.
- Figure clicks toggle; progress in the console; ✓ sends; a single figure choice sends on its click.
- Dead Men: companions highlighted, destination on the map.

## Acceptance criteria

- CC07; Stormcrow for the Free Peoples; Rage moving 0–4 units; excess removal after recruitment.
- No window for these decisions.

## Verification

- EditMode: figure choice model for the region panel.
- PlayMode: strategy card packs and layout of the region panel in four layouts; full EditMode.

## Documentation and handoff

Update: this file, `wotr-client:docs/ui/CARD_CHOICES_UX.md`, `wotr-client:docs/ui/BOARD_INTERACTION_UX.md`, `docs/TESTING.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## Result (2026-10-16)

`CardFigureDraft` and `CardFigureInteraction`: a card's figure choice (ArmyFigure: Denethor's Folly, Stormcrow;
ArmyFigures: The Grey Company, Hill-Trolls, Rage of the Dunlendings; the Dead Men's companions) and every
RemoveExcess outside a battle (one interface per decision kind) is made in the region panel. Regions holding
eligible figures glow; a click opens the panel, whose lit figures toggle within the card's limits. A choice of
exactly one figure is sent with its click; otherwise the console counts "Chosen n of m" and its ✓ confirms. The
Dead Men's destination stays a map click. The tray builds no figure buttons; excess after a battle stays in the
battle window.
