# UI-164 — Decision choices at their objects

<a id="cel"></a>
## Goal

Aggregate acceptance (OB01, extended by UI-210). According to the “permanent place of each control” principle (user decision with 2026-10-14), the choices that have the object pass from the decision window to this object. Since the user's request of 2026-10-16 ([CARD_CHOICES_UX](wotr-client:docs/ui/CARD_CHOICES_UX.md)), card decisions also keep their object-less options and Confirm choice in the turn console, so no base-game card decision opens the decision window.

<a id="kontekst-i-zależności"></a>
## Context and dependencies

After UI-160 (console and decision window). Paused by the user on 2026-10-14 (MAP-18) after client checkpoint 17dd5b6 (HasChoices without ↶, Use reaction in the card preview). Resumed as an aggregate on 2026-10-16: it closes after UI-211–UI-216. Inventory: [TURN_CONSOLE_UX](wotr-client:docs/ui/TURN_CONSOLE_UX.md), section E. MVP = base game; decisions expansions stay at their panels. No server changes, Contracts and rules; push only on request.

<a id="logika-i-zakres"></a>
## Logic and scope

- Select the deck at the dice Event selection: click the glowing deck at the hand.
- Canceling card selection: × preview card; reopen Meals: left slat (without a button in tray).
- card reaction outside battle and Hunt: Use/Skip in the card preview; reaction without card in the
  decision window.
- Effects cards with the choice of region, figures , reserves, nation, Companion or cards table:
  map, panel of the region, meals, Politics, Fellowship Characters, Effects.

<a id="kryteria-odbioru"></a>
## Acceptance criteria

- Each item of the inventory ‘UI-164’ operates on the object for both sides; the decision window
  does not show the choice that has the object.
- Scenario CC09: no base-game card decision opens the decision window (both sides), with UI-211–UI-216 done.
- After a card decision, the side that waits (also the player right after their own ✓) does not get a window
  holding only the committed effect trail (seen in UI-212: Nazgûl Search after the flight). Outside card
  decisions the trail still counts as a choice, because the battle window's host depends on it
  (`ShadowStrategy02NetworkTests.CorsairsRequiresAnotherRoundWhenDefenderStays`).
- The ui164 failure recorded before the pause is fixed: `Shadow04LayoutTests.ServerProjectedChoicesAndTwoArmiesFitBothSizesAndScales` (‘3 · One for the Dark Lord’ height 622.4 > 565.8).
- Regression cards (packages Strategy/Character from both sides), Buster, Politics, Characters and
  Fellowship without new failures.

## Result (2026-10-16)

Aggregate of UI-211–UI-216 (client `17bca0d`) plus clients `989a87f` and `46b286c`: base-game card decisions keep their choices on
their objects (map, region panel, Reserve, Effects panel, hand, dice) and in the turn console (options, progress, ✓),
and no longer open the decision window. CC09 for both sides: while the other side decides outside a battle and the
Hunt, the committed steps of the effect trail are no window choices, so the waiting side gets no window that only
recalls a card. The recorded `Shadow04LayoutTests` failure ("3 · One for the Dark Lord", reported as a height, in
fact an x overflow) was the Combat card's hover preview (UI-137), which overlays the map beside its tile by design;
`FellowshipLayoutTests.Check` exempts it from the tray's label check. Battles, the Hunt, the Fellowship panel and
Politics keep hosting their own decisions. Not run: a local build (lean verification preference: the user builds in
the Editor).
