# UI-214 — Card options and reactions in the console

## Goal

Client implementation: object-less card options (The Black Captain Commands order, The Nazgûl Strike! options, Lure of the Ring, There is Another Way, Dreadful Spells same-region alternatives) and object-less responses (Decline, Gandalf the Grey's draw, Balrog at a declaration, reveal outside the Hunt) are buttons in the console; The Lidless Eye confirms in the console.

## Context and dependencies

User request 2026-10-16: card choices about "what goes where" are handled in the fixed UI at the bottom with a Confirm choice option; one click on a highlighted figure, then distribute it on the board while the rules allow; fewest windows and clicks. Design: [CARD_CHOICES_UX](wotr-client:docs/ui/CARD_CHOICES_UX.md). Today these are buttons in the floating decision window (CardChoices.EffectButton, response list).

## Logic and scope

- Up to three option buttons replace the hint lines of the console's status zone; the console keeps its height; a click that completes the decision sends it.
- Card reactions with a card keep Use in the card preview (UI-164); Decline sits in the console.
- The Lidless Eye: dice in the pool, ✓ in the console.

## Acceptance criteria

- CC05 and CC06; Lure of the Ring for the Free Peoples; Ents follow-up Decline in the console.
- No option set of a base-game card exceeds three buttons, or the exception is documented.

## Verification

- EditMode: option strip model.
- PlayMode: Shadow character card packs and reaction tests; full EditMode.

## Result (2026-10-16)

`CardConsoleOptions` lists the console's buttons: the choices of The Black Captain Commands, Lure of the Ring (named
after its Companion), There is Another Way and The Nazgûl Strike! (Roll for the Hunt, Finish), Dreadful Spells'
alternatives once the region is chosen, and a reaction's responses whose source is not a card in the hand, plus
Decline, outside the Hunt, battles and event losses. `ConsoleStatus.SetOptions` shows up to three in the hint's place
(38 pt, the console keeps its height). The Nazgûl Strike!'s table cards are offered in the lit Effects panel
("Discard a table card" opens it at the first offered card); their × discards and sends. A reaction card in the hand
is lit and answers with its preview's Use. The tray builds none of these choices and the effect trail does not count
as a window choice for them, so no window opens. A reaction with more than three console buttons keeps the window
(none known in the base game). The Lidless Eye kept its dice and the console's ✓ (UI-211).
