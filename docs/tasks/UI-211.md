# UI-211 — Console confirm, undo and progress for card decisions

## Goal

Client implementation: the turn console's ✓ confirms and ↶ undoes card decisions whose choices are on objects or in the console; the status zone shows the card title and the draft's progress; the floating decision window stays closed for card decisions.

## Context and dependencies

User request 2026-10-16: card choices about "what goes where" are handled in the fixed UI at the bottom with a Confirm choice option; one click on a highlighted figure, then distribute it on the board while the rules allow; fewest windows and clicks. Design: [CARD_CHOICES_UX](wotr-client:docs/ui/CARD_CHOICES_UX.md). Today the console ✓ only checks a result or finishes an action sequence (TurnConsoleInteraction).

## Logic and scope

- ✓ is enabled for a card draft (EffectChoice, CharacterEvent, ArmyEvent, card RemoveExcess and Reaction) when the tray's preview is legal, and sends through the same confirm as the tray; tooltip "Confirm choice" with the result, or the reason when disabled.
- ↶ removes the draft's last step; with an empty draft it cancels as before.
- Status zone: headline with the card title, hint with progress (counts and remaining limits) from a presentation class without UI.
- DecisionWindow does not open for card decisions; the tray's own Confirm button is not built for them (the console owns it).

## Acceptance criteria

- Every card decision group of the inventory has an enabled ✓ when legal and a disabled ✓ with a reason otherwise.
- No card decision opens the decision window (test over the inventory's presentations).
- Region-only cards keep working with one map click.

## Verification

- EditMode: progress text and console state per draft kind.
- PlayMode: card packs for map-only and recruitment cards with ✓ in the console; full EditMode.

## Result (2026-10-16)

Client `e808514`: `CardDecisions` owns the player's EffectChoice, CharacterEvent, ArmyEvent and RemoveExcess
decisions outside a battle, the Hunt and event losses. The console's ✓ ("Confirm choice") is enabled when the
tray's preview is legal and sends through the same confirm; its tooltip gives the confirm text or the preview's
problem. The tray's Confirm no longer opens the decision window by itself (The Lidless Eye now keeps it closed).
The headline names the card and the hint leads with the progress. ↶ is the draft's undo. The tray keeps building
its Confirm for its own hosts and for decisions still shown in the window until UI-212–UI-216 move their choices.
