# UI-218 — Political map view

## Goal

Client implementation: a map mode that shows clearly which nation every region belongs to and which
settlements the other side has captured, switched on and off with a map button and a key.

## Context and dependencies

The owner's request of 2026-10-17 and their answers that day: regions in their nation's colour plus
captures marked in the captor's colour; a button with a keyboard shortcut, remembered between
sessions; armies, settlements and names stay, the illustration is subdued and play continues in the
view. MAP_CONTOURS_UX: the eight nation colours are never replaced by a Free Peoples/Shadow split,
and control has its own marker; the palette (`map-style.json`) and the nation of each region after
Fate and the White Hand (`RegionOverrides`) already reach the client, and `PlayerView.Control` gives
the current controller.

## Logic and scope

- A political layer between the terrain and the region highlights: every region with a nation is
  filled with that nation's colour; regions without a nation stay plain; the terrain is subdued.
- A region whose controller differs from its original owner (a captured settlement) gets stripes and
  an outline in the captor's colour (the Free Peoples sapphire or the Shadow ruby of the settlement
  gems), on top of its nation colour.
- Highlights of options, selection and hover, nation bands, armies, settlement gems and names draw
  above the layer, so every gesture works as in the normal view.
- A button above the map legend toggles the view (tooltip with the shortcut); the key P toggles it
  unless a text field has focus. The choice is stored on the device.
- The layer repaints only when a region's nation or controller changes; pan and zoom do not repaint it.
- The legend explains the view and its stripes; its outdated control symbols line is corrected.

## Acceptance criteria

- With the view on, all eight nations show in their palette colours, a captured settlement shows the
  captor's stripes, and the Fate/White Hand nation changes appear.
- The view survives a restart, and switching it repaints nothing while panning or zooming.
- Normal play (selecting armies, targets, hover) works unchanged in the view.

## Verification

- EditMode: stripe geometry inside region rings (also with holes), captor rule, stored preference.
- PlayMode where available; visual check in the owner's build of the next release.
