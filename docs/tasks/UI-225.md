# UI-225 — Battle window stays in place after "No card"

## Goal

Client fix: choosing "No card" in a battle keeps the battle window open where it is; it neither disappears for a
moment nor reopens somewhere else.

## Context and dependencies

Reported by the owner on 2026-10-17 in 0.9.0-rc.2: after "No card" the battle window vanishes briefly and
reappears in another place. The battle window (UI-115, BATTLE_WINDOW_UX) keeps one position during a battle;
UI-151 keeps "No card" in the footer while the opponent chooses.

## Logic and scope

- Find why the window closes and reopens after the card step (a decision change that rebuilds or re-docks the
  window, a lost position, a transient close) and keep the same window instance and position across the
  battle's decisions, including the wait for the opponent's card.
- No change to the battle's rules or commands.

## Acceptance criteria

- After "No card" (and after playing a card) the window stays visible and does not move until the battle ends
  or the player moves it.

## Verification

- PlayMode battle layout tests (window bounds before and after the card step); visual check in the next release.
