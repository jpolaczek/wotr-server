# UI-226 — Battle illustrations by nation

## Goal

Client implementation and artwork: the battle window's illustration for a town, city or stronghold depends on the
nation the place belongs to (Gondor, Rohan, the North, Dwarves, Elves, Isengard, Sauron, Southrons & Easterlings),
not only on its side.

## Context and dependencies

The owner's request of 2026-10-17: "we have battle pictures for cities and settlements depending on whose they are;
let's differentiate them by faction". UI-206 (BattleSceneArt, `graphicalassets/battles`) chooses
`<kind>-<free|shadow|wild>-<1|2>`. The board has 19 nation/settlement pairs, 20 with Fate (a dwarven City); the
nation is the current one (`RegionOverrides`).

## Logic and scope

- New illustrations `<town|city|stronghold>-<nation>-<1|2>`: two versions where the nation has two or more such
  places, otherwise one (30 images), in the UI-206 style (painted panorama, no text, original scenes).
- `BattleSceneArt` tries the nation's key first and falls back to the side key when an image is missing; versions
  alternate by region id within the nation's group, the same for both players.
- Open land and fortifications keep their UI-206 pictures.

## Acceptance criteria

- A battle at each nation's town, city or stronghold shows that nation's picture; a missing picture falls back to
  the side's.
- `build.py --check` passes and the art kit stays consistent.

## Verification

- EditMode: keys, versions and fallback order; `graphicalassets/battles/build.py --check`; visual check in the next
  release.
