# UI-219 — Fellowship route without the second click

## Goal

Client implementation: the Fellowship's declaration and revelation route no longer needs a second click on
its last region. A route that uses all of the Progress moves at once on its last step; a shorter route (or a
stay with Progress left) is sent with ✓ in the Fellowship's decision panel.

## Context and dependencies

The owner's request and choice of 2026-10-17 ("Full range sends, a shorter route with ✓"). The route stays
drawn step by step: it decides extra Hunt tiles (Shadow strongholds on the way) and Fire and Shadow, so the
game does not choose it (F03, BOARD_INTERACTION_UX). Character moves already go on one click (UI-105).

## Logic and scope

- A step that makes the route legal and leaves no further step (`route − 1 = Progress`) sends it at once,
  as a click on a reachable destination does for characters. With Progress 0, a click on the Fellowship's
  region stays and sends.
- A legal shorter route, and a stay with Progress left, wait for ✓ (`confirm-decision`), enabled by the
  server preview; Enter keeps sending it. Clicking the last region again does nothing.
- Undo step and Cancel stay; a reveal route ending in a sanctuary is still not legal and is not sent.

## Acceptance criteria

- Declaration and revelation at full range move without a second click; shorter routes move with ✓.
- No route is sent before it is legal; the hint and the player guide describe the new gesture.

## Verification

- EditMode: route completeness rule (full range, Progress 0 stay, shorter, sanctuary end).
- PlayMode helpers updated (FinishRoute uses ✓ for shorter routes); not run in CI.
- Visual check in the owner's build of the next release.
