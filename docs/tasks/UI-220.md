# UI-220 — Hide the Fellowship from its status plaque

## Goal

Client implementation: when the Fellowship is revealed and the player may hide it, the highlighted status
plaque that shows Hidden/Revealed is the control that hides it; the separate Hide button goes away.

## Context and dependencies

The owner's request of 2026-10-17: "do not hide it with an extra button; the highlighted panel that shows
hidden is the button". The action and its cost (the die the server allows) do not change.

## Logic and scope

- While hiding is a legal option, the Fellowship status plaque is highlighted as clickable (UI-113 pressed
  state, tooltip naming the action and the die) and one click hides the Fellowship, like other one-click
  actions (UI-142).
- When hiding is not legal, the plaque is not clickable; no other control is added.
- The former Hide button is removed from the Fellowship panel and the tray.

## Acceptance criteria

- A revealed Fellowship with a legal hide option is hidden by one click on its status plaque.
- No separate Hide button remains; the plaque is not clickable without a legal option.

## Verification

- EditMode where the rule is pure; PlayMode helpers updated; visual check in the next release.
