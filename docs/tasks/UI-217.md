# UI-217 — Readable card names in the hand tiles

## Goal

Client implementation: every card name in the collapsed hand tiles (bottom-right Character/Strategy
tray) is fully readable, without clipped lines, in every supported layout. The tray keeps its look.

## Context and dependencies

Found by the owner on 2026-10-17 in 0.9.0-rc.2 on Linux: long names such as "The Black Captain
Commands" wrap to three lines in a tile whose title holds two (`#hand-card-title`, `max-height:
30px`, vertically centred), so the first and last lines are cut. The tile is built by `HandCard`
and laid out by `HandView` (46 px tiles, 40 px with `compact-edge-board`, two or three columns).
No text in the tile shrinks to fit today, and the PlayMode check "Title clipped" in
`CardInteractionTests` measures the slot button's empty text instead of the title label.

## Logic and scope

- The tile title keeps its font, colour and two-line height. A name that needs more than two lines
  at the normal size gets a smaller font size, just enough for two lines, never below 8 px; the
  longest word always fits one line. Short names keep the normal size.
- The size follows the title's width: resizing the window, the compact board, UI scale 125%, three
  columns of an excess hand and the battle's combat titles recompute it.
- No other panel changes; the unrolled card keeps its full title.

## Acceptance criteria

- No base-game or expansion card name is clipped in the collapsed tile at 1366×768 and 1920×1080,
  scale 100% and 125%, two and three columns.
- Names that fit two lines at the normal size are unchanged.

## Verification

- EditMode: the font-size rule (fits, shrinks, minimum, longest word, idempotent after shrinking).
- PlayMode: `CardInteractionTests` checks the title label of every tile against its height at both
  sizes and scales (not run in CI; the self-hosted runner runs it).
- Visual check of the longest names in the owner's build after the next release.
