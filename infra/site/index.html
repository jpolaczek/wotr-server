The game

War of the Ring is a strategy board game for two players set in J.R.R. Tolkien’s The Lord of the Rings. Action dice decide what each side can do in a turn: muster and move armies, play event cards, bring heroes into play — or move the Fellowship one step closer to Mount Doom while the Shadow hunts it.

The Free Peoples

Bring the Ring-bearers through Mordor to Mount Doom and destroy the One Ring before its corruption takes them — or turn the war around and take the Shadow’s strongholds by force of arms.

The Shadow

Conquer the cities and strongholds of the free nations, or hunt the Fellowship until the Ring-bearers fall to its corruption. Every die you spend on the Hunt is a die your armies cannot use.

The expansions

Play the base game, or add any of the three expansions when you set up a table, alone or together. The server applies each expansion’s rules, and the game shows its characters, dice and cards on the board. Like the base game, each expansion is a paid board game from Ares Games.

  • Lords of Middle-earth

    Elrond and Galadriel bring the Elven Rings Narya, Nenya and Vilya into the war. The Shadow answers with Gothmog, the Balrog and new versions of its Minions, and Sméagol can join the Fellowship. With new Event cards, and the Council of Rivendell variant, in which the Free Peoples choose alternative Companions.

  • Kings of Middle-earth

    Five Sovereigns, Brand, Dáin, Denethor, Théoden and Thranduil, can be Awakened as Companions or Corrupted by the Shadow. Ruler dice and Ruler tokens add new choices, and Dark Chieftains such as the Black Serpent and Uglúk join the Shadow’s armies. With new Event cards and the Dismay and Removing Ruler Tokens variants.

  • The Fate of Erebor

    The war in the North changes: Erebor begins in Sauron’s hands and the Dwarves are active from the start, with Gimli the Dwarven Lord among them. Retaking the Lonely Mountain is worth two points. With new cards and the New Cities variant.

The online version

  • Rules checked by the server

    Every move is validated by the game server, which also rolls the dice. Hidden information stays hidden: you never see your opponent’s hand.

  • Live or by correspondence

    Play in one sitting or a move a day. Each decision is saved on the server, so you can close the game and continue later on any of your computers.

  • The board, not menus

    Choose on the map, the dice and the panels: click a region, a figure, a portrait or a card. Figures behave like miniatures: click one to lift it or drag it onto a lit target. Every character ability has its own icon, and one click uses it. The game lights up what you can do right now and explains it.

  • See what your opponent does

    Their moves play on the board step by step: the die they spend, armies marching, attacking and capturing, cards played and the Hunt. One click skips ahead, and the same view works for observers and replays.

  • Ranked games

    Find game pairs you with a player whose choices fit yours: the expansions, your side and the pace. Every move has a time limit, each player has three pauses, and a ranked game is played to its end. Your rating follows the result, and the Leaderboards show the top 25.

  • Your avatar

    A small picture stands beside your name in chat, at the table and when a ranked opponent is presented. Every account starts with the Eye or the Ring, and on your account page you can choose a soldier of any nation instead.

  • Open games and challenges

    See the open tables and who is online, talk in the chat, and right-click a player’s name to send a challenge. When they accept, a private game opens.

  • Private tables

    Invite a friend with a table code and agree on the setup together. Friends can watch as observers; in the board’s chat they read everyone, while players only read each other.

  • Replays

    Save a finished game, watch it again move by move, and export it to share it.

  • Try it alone

    A demo against a simple computer opponent lets you learn the board before you invite anyone.

  • Updates itself

    Install once: the game downloads new versions by itself and can repair its installation.

Installation

macOS
  1. Open the downloaded .pkg file. The installer is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS says it cannot check it. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find the message about the installer and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password.
  3. In the installer choose Install for me only. The game goes to Applications in your home folder and later updates will not ask for a password.
  4. The game starts after the installation. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
Windows
  1. Run the downloaded WotrOnline-…-win-Setup.exe. If your browser asks about the file, choose Keep.
  2. The installer is not signed by Microsoft yet, so Windows may show Windows protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. No administrator rights are needed. The game installs for your user, adds shortcuts to the desktop and the Start menu, starts by itself and later updates itself. Uninstall it in Settings → Apps.
  4. With Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks unsigned installers and the game cannot be installed for now. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
Linux and Steam Deck (experimental)
  1. Save the downloaded .AppImage file in a folder of your own, for example ~/Applications: updates replace this file.
  2. Make it executable: right-click → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing, or run chmod +x WotrOnline-*.AppImage.
  3. Start it by double-clicking. If it does not start and mentions FUSE, install your distribution’s fuse3 package. The game updates itself in place.
  4. Steam Deck: in Desktop Mode do the steps above, then add the file to Steam with Add a Non-Steam Game to play it in Gaming Mode. Use Steam + X for the on-screen keyboard when signing in.

Start playing

  1. Create an account. Register on this site and confirm your email with the link we send. You sign in to the game with your account name.
  2. Set a table. In the game open Tables, create a table and send its invitation to your opponent.
  3. Play. Agree on the setup, both accept it, and the host starts the game.

The board game

War of the Ring is a board game designed by Roberto Di Meglio, Marco Maggi and Francesco Nepitello, with art by John Howe, created, published and distributed worldwide by Ares Games, who also publish its expansions Lords of Middle-earth, Kings of Middle-earth and The Fate of Erebor. The base game and each expansion are paid products, sold by Ares Games and game stores. This online version is free and open source; it exists thanks to their work and with great respect for it. If you enjoy it, please buy the board game and its expansions from Ares Games or your local game store.
